Reel 01 · Grainy, Half-Remembered

Where It All Began

2024 – 2025

Every journey has a starting point, and this is ours.

Back in 2024, we would occasionally see each other in a YouTube live chat. Our conversations were nothing more than simple "hello" and "hi" messages. At the time, they felt ordinary, and neither of us knew those small interactions would eventually lead us here.

Everything changed in December 2024.

At 9:16 PM, during a YouTube membership gifting event, I noticed that you weren't able to receive a gifted membership. I thought it might be because you were using an iPhone, so I decided to message you and help. It was a small conversation with a simple intention, but it became the moment that started our journey.

A month later, on 19 January 2025, we had our first proper conversation. We talked about how things were going, what was happening around us, and mostly about roleplay scenarios and the server. There was nothing extraordinary about those conversations, but they were honest, comfortable, and effortless.

As days turned into weeks, we continued talking. Message by message, we got to know each other a little better. Without realizing it, a genuine friendship was beginning to grow.

Looking back now, it's amazing how one small message, sent with the intention of helping someone, became the first chapter of a story that means so much to both of us.

Reel 06 · Just Us

One Month, and Still Falling

Today

A month ago, this was just a story about how we started. Now it's harder to write, only because there's so much I want to say and none of it feels like enough.

This one month with you has been beautiful in a way I didn't expect. Not loud, not dramatic, just steady, warm, and completely yours. You've become my favorite person to talk to, my favorite person to laugh with, and somehow, my biggest support system too. On the days I doubt myself, you're the one who reminds me I don't have to.

And the strange, wonderful thing is this: the more days go by, the more I fall for you. Not less. Not the same. More.

So here's to you, baby: for every "hello" that turned into something none of us saw coming, for choosing me, and for every page still left to write.
