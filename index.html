Still Us.

I know our conversations got a lot more intimate right after we got engaged — and I was the one steering us there. I get why that felt like a lot, and maybe a little different from the "me" you know.

But nothing about how I feel about you has changed. We just leaned too far one way, too fast. Before anything else, I'm still your friend — the one who annoys you, laughs with you, and talks about nothing in particular.

the one month is fully respected — no pressure, ever

Thank you. 🐾

— still yours, always me
