a letter, from me to you

Hey you,

I think we've been thinking too much lately — so I put it all down properly, for you.

I. Before Everything Changed

We were friends, first.

We laughed at the stupidest things. We had random talks, shared our overthinking, and felt so comfortable with each other that nothing needed explaining. Those days mean a great deal to me.

II. Then Something New

The moment we called ourselves "us."

Things naturally became more intimate between us — we started talking about more personal, intimate things, more often than before. It happened quickly, and quietly, without either of us really deciding it.

III. A Small Confession

I talked about it too much.

I asked more questions, brought it up more often than I should have, and maybe made it feel like something about me had changed. If that made you uncomfortable, I am genuinely sorry.

IV. What Hasn't Changed

I didn't stop being your friend.

Not for a single day. I still want to laugh with you, annoy you a little, listen to you, talk about nothing in particular, and be exactly the person you have always known.

V. Why I Actually Fell For You

Not for any of that.

I fell for you because of who you actually are — a genuine person, with a big heart, who cares for me without keeping score. You're the one who listens to all my nonsense and never once made me feel small for it. That is everything.

VI. What I Actually Want

Only these, really.

Your Comfort
You should always feel safe with me.
Our Friendship
The heart of everything.
Honest Talks
Not assumed. Not overthought.
No Pressure
Never, for anything.
Us
That's all I really want.
VII. The Truth Is
  • Nothing is broken.
  • Nothing needs to be rushed.
  • I'm still here, and I'm still me.
  • I still care about you, just as much.
